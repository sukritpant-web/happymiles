A new name, with the right paperwork and the right people.

Happy Miles Tours and Travels Pvt. Ltd. is a registered travel agency based in Kaldhara, Kathmandu.

Our story

Just getting started — and proud of it.

We founded Happy Miles to do travel in Nepal a little differently: honestly, personally, and in genuine partnership with the guides, drivers and hotels who make a trip memorable.

Being new means we don't have a back-catalogue of packages to push. Instead, every journey we arrange is built from scratch around the traveller in front of us. It also means we get to build our partner network the right way from day one — on fair, transparent terms.

"Happy Miles exists to make travel in Nepal feel personal — for the people who visit, and the people who host them."
