Nepal Visa Information

Everything you need to know about Nepal tourist visa

Tourist Visa Fees

15 Days USD 30
30 Days USD 50
90 Days USD 125

Visa on arrival available at Tribhuvan International Airport

Requirements

  • Passport valid for 6+ months
  • 2 passport photos
  • Completed application form
  • Payment in USD

Free Visa

  • SAARC citizens (30 days)
  • Chinese nationals
  • Children under 10
